# GauTrack: questions for the department

Rewari district stray cattle registry. Prepared for the office of the District Municipal
Commissioner.

Seventeen questions, numbered straight through and grouped by who can answer them, so each
block can be forwarded whole.
None of them are software questions. Two of them, marked below, change what gets built next;
the rest inform the plan and can be answered in parallel.

## 1. What the office must arrange

Errands, not decisions. All seven can be started today.

| # | What is needed | Note |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | A web address, such as `cattle.rewari.gov.in` | A `gov.in` name goes through the IT cell; a `.in` name costs about ₹1,000 a year and takes ten minutes. Either works, and they can be swapped later |
| 2 | One computer that stays on permanently | Either a machine in the office with a fixed public address and a battery backup, or one rented in an Indian data centre for a few thousand rupees a month |
| 3 | That machine registered to the department | Office email, office payment method. Not a private individual's account |
| 4 | A list of who needs a login | Name, mobile number, and one of: field officer, office, read-only. **One account each; a shared login makes the audit trail worthless** |
| 5 | Android phones with mobile data for field staff | Their own phones are fine. Nothing is installed |
| 6 | One officer formally responsible for the data | A signature, not a workload. Needed before real names are entered |
| 7 | A security inspection by a government-approved auditor | Required for any system holding citizens' details. Slow, so start the request early |

## 2. For the Animal Husbandry department

Deputy Director, Animal Husbandry, Rewari. Telephone **01274-225936**.

| # | Question | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **How many animals in Rewari district already carry the 12-digit Pashu Aadhaar tag?** | **Changes what gets built.** Every tagged animal is work we do not repeat. Only a departmental login can see this: Bharat Pashudhan publishes national totals and nothing else, with no district breakdown and no public tag lookup |
| 2 | Can that list be exported for the district's own use? | Decides whether the register starts from existing records or from zero |
| 3 | Which benefits does a cattle keeper here actually receive: subsidy, insurance, shed licence? | A penalty that costs a benefit is deducted, not demanded. Fines alone are being ignored |

## 3. For the Municipal Council

| # | Question | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| 4 | **Of animals actually picked up, how many are cows and how many are bulls or male calves?** | **Changes what gets built,** and it is the number nobody has. A cow goes home to an owner and the owner can be fined. A bull or male calf was abandoned because it gives no milk, will never be claimed, and needs a permanent place costing money every year. The ratio decides whether this plan is mainly enforcement or mainly shelter |
| 5 | Is the ~4,500 stray figure for Rewari city trusted, and does it cover the whole municipal area? | Every promise, cost and shelter calculation is a multiple of it. It is a self-reported municipal survey figure, never checked against a count |
| 6 | What happened to the 29 keepers identified as releasing animals? | They were identified in August 2026 and no action followed. If identification was not the obstacle, a better identification system will not fix it |
| 7 | What rule sets the ₹5,100 release charge, and has it ever been challenged? | The catching tender rate is ₹1,880, and Haryana municipal law appears to cap this kind of penalty considerably lower. A charge that cannot be defended sits at the centre of the plan |
| 8 | What happens today to an animal nobody claims? | Formalising what already happens is cheaper than introducing something new, and it shows where animals actually go |
| 9 | Who does the catching: council staff or the contractor, and can using the system be written into the contract? | Whoever handles the animal has to enter the record, or the register is always second-hand |
| 10 | What is spent on stray-cattle work each year, and where does it go? | The strongest case is "the same money, with a register behind it", not "fund something new". Roughly ₹34 lakh was reported for 2026 capture operations |

## 4. For the Haryana Gau Seva Aayog

| # | Question | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| 11 | Current occupancy and sanctioned capacity of each district gaushala, in writing | The number of animals that can leave the road this month is the number of free places. The published total of 5,252 has not moved across three quarters, which usually means it is not being updated |
| 12 | Karauli, 62 acres, announced January 2026: what capacity, and when does it open? | The largest single piece of new capacity anywhere in the plan. No figure or date has been published |

## 5. For the Commissioner and the office

| # | Question | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| 13 | When shelters are full, what is the catching team told to do? | Without an answer the plan stops at the shelter gate. An officer with nowhere to put an animal releases it again |
| 14 | How many people will enter records, and are they comfortable using a phone? | Decides how much training is needed and how simple the screens must stay |
| 15 | Who reads the numbers each morning? | A dashboard nobody has been told to read stops being true within a month, because nobody notices when entries stop arriving |
| 16 | Whose approval makes this official: Commissioner, Deputy Commissioner, or the state department? | Decides the order everything else happens in |

## 7. The two to answer first

Everything above is useful. These two change the software rather than inform it.

| Question | If the answer is one thing | If it is the other |
|---|---|---|
| **Q1** How many already carry the tag | Mostly tagged: the field app becomes a lookup-and-verify tool and the register starts from existing records | Mostly untagged: the field app is a registration tool and tagging is a year of work |
| **Q4** Cows against bulls and male calves | Mostly cows: the plan is enforcement, and fines can carry it | Mostly males: the plan is shelter capacity, and no fine will move the number on the road |
